# Business Context — If Walls Could Talk LLC

## Owner and business model
- Owner: Heather, If Walls Could Talk LLC
- Service area: Brevard County / Space Coast, Florida
- Model: Heather sets standards and reviews estimates; vetted local crews perform work
- Positioning: homeowner education first, transparent process, no hard-sell tactics

## Current operating context
- Heather is currently out of the country and returning in October 2026
- Immediate objective is lead flow now, with quality control through trusted crews
- Site status: indexing enabled for production (2026-06-03); submit sitemap in Google Search Console after deploy

## Voice and trust posture
- Direct, plain, practical, and honest
- Educational, not salesy
- Calls out real failure points homeowners face in Florida (humidity, UV, salt air, mold risk)
- Signature messaging: prep quality determines outcomes

## Five-layer business vision
1. **Lead generation now:** Website content educates and converts into local calls/texts.
2. **October return + SOP build:** Document repeatable standards and job workflows.
3. **Worker training system:** SOP-backed worker support/chatbot for consistency at scale.
4. **Homeowner chatbot / subscription:** Site content becomes productized guidance for DIY and homeowner support.
5. **Reentry hiring pathway:** Build a structured second-chance workforce model using SOPs and documented training.

## Why this matters strategically
- Website content serves multiple purposes beyond marketing:
  - immediate trust and leads
  - future worker training data
  - future homeowner knowledge product
- Decisions made now should preserve flexibility for the reentry/training model later.

## Existing strengths
- Strong word-of-mouth foundation and local credibility
- Real personal alignment with veteran and reentry communities
- Clear brand tone and regional relevance
